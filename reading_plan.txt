1. NOW - JAN 2027                                              [ ] not started
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THESIS FOCUS
  Chapter 1: governance-conditioned EVPI and EVSI

READING AND SPECIFIC SECTIONS
  - Raiffa & Schlaifer, Applied Statistical Decision Theory, Part II
    ("Additive Utility, Opportunity Loss, and the Value of Information");
    chapters on EVPI/EVSI with two/three processes.
  - Bolam et al. (2019), Biological Reviews 94: 629-647 (full review).
  - Jaynes, Probability Theory: The Logic of Science
    (priors, Bayesian updating, sampling theory).
  - Boyd & Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Ch. 1-5.

TOPICS TO HIT
  - Bayesian updating; prior/posterior beliefs
  - Expected utility and risk neutrality
  - Opportunity loss
  - EVPI derivation (2-state, 2-action)
  - EVSI and EVPI relationship
  - Imperfect information and sampling
  - VoI reporting conventions in conservation
  - EVPI/EVSI in ecological language
  - Convex sets and functions
  - Constrained optimisation formulation
  - Duality basics
  - Governance feasibility sets and thresholds
  - Deterministic vs probabilistic approval

WHAT TO EXTRACT / FOCUS ON
  From Raiffa & Schlaifer: the opportunity-loss logic behind EVPI/EVSI, not
  the conjugate-prior machinery. From Bolam: how VoI is reported in
  conservation and the gaps the governance framing fills. From Jaynes:
  posterior updating and sampling. From Boyd: enough convex formulation to
  model governance feasibility sets and constrained approval.

OUTPUT
  Derive deterministic and probabilistic EVPI_G; begin EVSI_G; prove simple
  bounds; build clean R simulation for the toy problem.

3. JUL - DEC 2027                                              [ ] not started
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THESIS FOCUS
  Chapter 2: multi-objective decision-makers

READING AND SPECIFIC SECTIONS
  - Abbas, Foundations of Multiattribute Utility (core chapters: additive
    utility, multiplicative utility, preference independence, behavioural
    assumptions).
  - Gibbons, Game Theory for Applied Economists, Ch. 1-2
    (Nash equilibrium, strategic form).

TOPICS TO HIT
  - Additive utility and value functions
  - Multiplicative utility and scaling constant k
  - Preference independence
  - Behavioural assumptions and failures
  - Non-compensatory constraints as feasibility filters
  - Nash equilibrium
  - Strategic-form games
  - Multiple actors and weight vectors
  - Trade-off sensitivity
  - Objective conflict and information non-actionability

WHAT TO EXTRACT / FOCUS ON
  Abbas: how additive MAUT fails under preference dependence; how the
  multiplicative form captures interacting attributes; how to justify
  non-compensatory constraints as feasibility filters. Gibbons: basic
  equilibrium concepts for multi-actor governance.

OUTPUT
  Build RQ2 toy models with multiple actors and weight vectors; formalise
  trade-off sensitivity and objective conflict; draft Chapter 2 model.

5. JUL - DEC 2028                                              [ ] not started

THESIS FOCUS
  Chapter 3: iterative multi-actor governance

READING AND SPECIFIC SECTIONS
  - Krishnamurthy, Ch. 2-3 (Bayesian filtering, controlled sensing);

  - Lange, Applied Probability, Ch. 6-7 (Markov chains, Poisson processes).

  - Baarslag & Kaisers (2017), value of information in automated negotiation.

TOPICS TO HIT
  - Bayesian filtering
  - Controlled sensing
  - Structural results for POMDPs
  - Stopping-time POMDPs
  - Markov chains
  - Poisson processes
  - Sequential VoI
  - Epistemic constraints
  - Negotiation trajectories
  - Joint state/signal/belief updates
  - Dynamic feasibility sets

WHAT TO EXTRACT / FOCUS ON
  Krishnamurthy: belief updating, observation models, and structural results
  for sequential VoI. Lange: Markov-chain transitions for ecological and
  socio-economic state evolution. Li & Pozzi: VoI in a sequential POMDP with
  epistemic constraints. Baarslag & Kaisers: how information shapes the
  trajectory of negotiation, not just one decision.

OUTPUT
  Build RQ3 POMDP model with joint ecological/socio-economic states, joint
  signals, and dynamic feasibility sets; run simulations; draft Paper 3.
